I build things that most people keep in separate departments. My work crosses hardware, embedded systems, robotics, mobile software, AI agent architecture, and visual production — not because I couldn't specialize, but because the interesting problems live at the intersections.

Co-founder and principal engineer at Redstone Laboratories, an applied engineering lab building custom robotics integration for warehouse and agricultural clients in New England. I also lead software at LIDI, our software arm, and work as iOS developer on ResiLife for Elite Home Fitness Solution.

I'm completing an electrical engineering degree at FIU while running production systems. My long-term interest is in AI with genuine physical presence — autonomous agents with real mechanical bodies, operating in the real world. That work is already underway.

Everything in this space is mine. Some of it is finished. Most of it is still becoming.
